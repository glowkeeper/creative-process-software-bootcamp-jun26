## Big Idea

> Good software is improved through iteration.

Your project does not need to be polished or complete. Today is about making
deliberate choices, improving what matters and preparing to explain your work.

By the end of the session, aim to have:

- made one meaningful improvement
- tested the most important part of your project
- attempted a production build and deployment
- prepared another demonstration route if deployment is unavailable
- rehearsed a presentation of no more than five minutes
